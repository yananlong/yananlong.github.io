I am a machine learning researcher and consulting CTO at [StickFlux Labs](https://stickflux.com/), working on AI evaluation, Bayesian statistics and participatory AI governance. I study what evaluation results can support, how reporting choices affect conclusions, and how uncertainty and stakeholder perspectives should inform decisions about AI systems.

My recent work includes two ICML 2026 papers on [social-impact evaluation reporting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05613) and [benchmark saturation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16763), the [Evaluation Cards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09809) and [Every Eval Ever](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14516) preprints, and a [Bayesian audit of public frontier-AI evaluation archives](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.17005). I also work on multilingual mechanistic interpretability and the relationship between technical evidence and participatory governance.

My background is in biomedical machine learning, including drug–drug interaction prediction, wearable time-series analysis and reproducible training pipelines. I received my PhD in Chemistry (Biomedical Data Science) from the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/), where I also worked as a researcher data scientist.

Since 2023, I have been a core organizer at [Queer in AI](https://www.queerinai.com/). Alongside technical research, I use participatory design and critical HCI to examine how communities shape AI evaluation and governance.
