I am a machine learning researcher and consulting CTO working on AI evaluation, Bayesian statistics and biomedical ML. My research spans benchmark validity, evaluation reporting, uncertainty quantification and participatory AI governance.

## Experience

**StickFlux Labs — Consulting CTO**

Research and consulting on AI evaluation and reproducible ML. Recent work includes social-impact evaluation reporting, benchmark saturation, Bayesian audits of public evaluation archives and multilingual mechanistic interpretability. Engineering experience includes training, hyperparameter-tuning and versioning pipelines for multivariate time-series classification.

**University of Chicago — Researcher Data Scientist**

Biomedical data science and machine learning, including frailty prediction from accelerometry and clinical covariates, drug–drug interaction prediction, and Bayesian analysis of population health data.
