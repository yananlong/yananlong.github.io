I am a machine learning researcher and consulting CTO. **Research areas:** AI evaluations, Bayesian statistics, Trustworthy AI, AI for Science. My research spans benchmark validity, evaluation reporting, uncertainty quantification and participatory AI governance.

## Experience

**StickFlux Labs — Consulting CTO**

Research and consulting on AI evaluation and reproducible ML. Recent work includes social-impact evaluation reporting, benchmark saturation, Bayesian audits of public evaluation archives and multilingual mechanistic interpretability. Engineering experience includes training, hyperparameter-tuning and versioning pipelines for multivariate time-series classification.

**University of Chicago — Researcher Data Scientist**

Biomedical data science and machine learning, including frailty prediction from accelerometry and clinical covariates, drug–drug interaction prediction, and Bayesian analysis of population health data.

## Education

- **PhD, Chemistry (Biomedical Data Science)**, University of Chicago, 2023. [Dissertation](https://doi.org/10.6082/uchicago.7603).
- **BA, Chemistry (Distinction) and Mathematics**, Music minor, *cum laude*, Colby College, 2016.
- **Visiting student**, St Edmund Hall, University of Oxford, 2015. Tutorials in Biochemistry and Mathematics.

## Technical skills

- **Programming and data:** Python, R, C/C++, Julia, Scala, Java, Bash, Perl, SQL and Spark.
- **Statistics:** Bayesian modelling, uncertainty quantification and calibration with Stan, PyMC and NumPyro.
- **Machine learning:** PyTorch, JAX/Flax, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn, Transformers, PyTorch Geometric and gradient boosting.

- **Languages:** English, Chinese (Mandarin and Cantonese), German and French.
